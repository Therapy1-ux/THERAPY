THERAPY™ Biomarker Program

Your gut, hormones, mood and energy are one conversation. Start with the data.

Private blood testing, curated by our clinical team and reviewed with you in a consult at our Eltham centre. No GP referral needed to order.

Choose your panel → How it works

Testing powered by i-screen · Samples processed in NATA-accredited Australian laboratories.

A number on a report isn't an answer. The conversation about it is.

Your gut, hormones, mood and energy don't work in isolation, so we don't read them that way. We look at your markers together, alongside your sleep, stress, food, movement and stage of life.

Every panel ends with a consult. We explain what your results show and what they don't, and agree on practical next steps with you. If anything needs medical follow-up, we'll point you to your GP.

FAQ

Questions.

Do I need a GP referral?+

No. i-screen issues the pathology request when you order, so you don’t need a referral.

Can I claim it on Medicare?+

No. These are private tests and aren’t covered by Medicare.

Who looks at my results?+

Your results are released to you in your i-screen dashboard. With your consent, they are also shared with THERAPY™ so our [PRACTITIONER TITLE] can go through them with you in your consult.

Where is blood taken?+

At a participating pathology collection centre. i-screen shows you the centres near you when you order.

Is my data private?+

Yes. Results are shared between i-screen and THERAPY™ only if you tick the consent box at checkout, and are handled under our privacy policy.
