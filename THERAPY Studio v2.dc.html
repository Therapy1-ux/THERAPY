The idea

Breath, movement, sound, stillness and contrast — in one room.

Five inputs, sequenced to do one job: calm the nervous system, release held tension, restore balance. Pilates, yoga and strength are the shapes the work takes — the state you leave in is the point.

A space to pause, reconnect, and walk out renewed rather than emptied.

Immersive by design

The room is part of the method.

Four environmental layers, programmed to the session — the same discipline we apply to a protocol downstairs.

LIGHT

Circadian-tuned — amber and low at dawn, cool through the strength block, near-dark for the close.

SOUND

Transformative, not background. A scored spatial system carries the room from arrival through effort into stillness.

HEAT

Infrared-assisted warmth held to the class — hot when the work calls for it, neutral when the breath does.

SCENT

One house accord, dosed low. Your nervous system learns the room and settles faster each time.

Community & connection

You regulate faster
in good company.

Co-regulation is real physiology. Breathing in time with a room full of people settles the nervous system faster than doing it alone — which is why every session is held together, start to finish, rather than run on individual timers.

Small capacity, familiar faces, the same coaches each week. You arrive as a name, not a booking.

This isn't something a wearable can score. Progress here is a settled nervous system and how you actually feel walking out — steadier, less braced, more yourself — read alongside the data, never instead of it.

New · Curated experiences

The Exhale

A day retreat. Disconnect to reconnect — without leaving the city.

Melbourne's retreats ask for a two-hour drive and an overnight bag, or hand you a robe and leave you to soak. The Exhale is neither: one structured day of guided practice — movement, mindset, stillness — held in sequence, in the studio. No ceremony for ceremony's sake. Just a full day of the work, done properly, together. And a reminder that enjoying whatever movement you choose counts.
