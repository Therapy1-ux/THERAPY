The modalities

The science behind each therapy.

These are the building blocks we sequence into your protocol — each chosen for what it does to your body, not just how it feels.

Recovery · Focus

Hydrogen Ice Bath

Cold immersion clamps the blood vessels shut; rewarming then floods your tissue with fresh, oxygen-rich circulation — a vascular pump that clears inflammation, sharpens focus and trains a calmer response to stress. Hydrogen nanobubbles add antioxidant support to speed the bounce-back.

•Evidence-based — peer-reviewed research indexed on PubMed
Relaxation · Detox

PBM Bed & Infrared Light Therapy

Lie back while full-body photobiomodulation and far infrared work together. Infrared warms you from the inside out, lifting core temperature so heart rate and circulation climb — cardiovascular conditioning close to a gentle workout — while red and near-infrared light is absorbed by your mitochondria to support cellular energy and repair. Muscles let go, detox pathways open through the sweat response, and the nervous system shifts toward rest. The infrared and red/near-infrared wavelengths are tuned to specific frequencies (bands within roughly 630–850nm) chosen because that range is what penetrates tissue and drives the mitochondrial response — it isn't generic heat, it's frequency-targeted light.

•Evidence-based — peer-reviewed research indexed on PubMed
Skin · Repair
