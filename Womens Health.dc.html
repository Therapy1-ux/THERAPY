Our approach

Women's health should never be reduced to one hormone, one symptom or one stage of life.

For many women, the changes begin years before menopause — and they take those changes seriously here. Our care looks beyond individual symptoms to the interaction between them.

One clinical picture. Connected care for the whole woman.

Where we're different

Your mental health belongs in the menopause conversation.

Perimenopause is often presented as hot flushes and changing periods. But for many women, the symptoms that bring them looking for help are happening in their brain and nervous system.

At THERAPY™, mental health isn't separated from women's health. It is part of women's health.

Whole-woman assessment

The symptoms can reach far beyond your period.

Every woman's presentation is different. That is precisely why care needs to be individualised.
