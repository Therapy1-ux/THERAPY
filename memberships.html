Advanced neuro & cellular therapies

ExoMind™ and ReOxy, explained.

Magnetic brain stimulation

ExoMind™

Levels 3 & 4

A non-invasive brain-stimulation treatment using focused magnetic pulses (TMS) directed at the prefrontal cortex — the region that governs mood, focus, impulse control and decision-making. Sessions are about 30 minutes; you sit comfortably, awake, with no downtime.

Benefits

Each session gently activates under-active brain networks. Repeated over a course, the brain strengthens those pathways (neuroplasticity) — so benefits build gradually, typically over 4–6 weeks of regular sessions.

✦Mood & motivation
Lifts low mood and restores drive
✦Focus & attention
Sharper concentration, less mental fog
✦Calmer stress response
Quieter mind, less reactivity
✦Emotional regulation
More pause between trigger and reaction
✦Sleep quality
Easier wind-down, deeper rest
✦Drug-free & non-invasive
No medication, no anaesthetic, no downtime
Who it’s for
CEOs & high performers
For leaders carrying a heavy cognitive load — long days, constant decisions, little recovery. ExoMind™ supports mental clarity, sustained focus and composure under pressure, helping you think sharply in the boardroom and switch off afterwards.
Athletes
Performance starts in the brain. Supports focus, reaction under pressure, confidence and emotional control on game day — and calmer, better-quality rest between sessions.
Brain fog
For that heavy, foggy, “can’t think straight” feeling after burnout, illness, poor sleep or hormonal change. Stimulating the prefrontal cortex helps restore mental energy, clarity and recall.
ADHD & ASD
Support for focus, emotional regulation and sensory overwhelm — helping the brain’s “control centre” stay online.
Depression
TMS is an established, evidence-based approach for depression. ExoMind™ builds on that science to support mood and motivation.
Anxiety
Helps calm an over-active stress response and quiet racing thoughts.
OCD
In OCD, the brain’s “error alarm” — a loop between the prefrontal cortex and deeper brain regions — keeps firing, driving intrusive thoughts and the urge to repeat rituals. Magnetic stimulation is used to help settle that loop, so thoughts feel less “sticky” and compulsions easier to resist. TMS is an approved treatment pathway for OCD in several countries; it works best alongside therapy such as ERP.
Eating disorders
Binge eating, bulimia and restrictive patterns are linked to the brain’s reward and self-control networks. Strengthening prefrontal control may help reduce urges, food preoccupation and the anxiety around eating. Research is growing and encouraging; ExoMind™ supports — never replaces — your treating psychologist, dietitian and GP.
Hormonal change
Mood, sleep and cognitive shifts through perimenopause, menopause and other hormonal transitions.
Emerging research
Early studies are exploring TMS in Parkinson’s, dementia and MS — for movement, memory and fatigue. Promising, but still emerging.
Alternating oxygen breathing

ReOxy IHHT

Level 3 · Level 4

Intermittent hypoxic-hyperoxic training. You rest in a chair breathing through a mask while the air alternates between low and high oxygen. That gentle, controlled stress prompts your cells to renew their mitochondria — the engines that make energy — so the body uses oxygen more efficiently. No exercise required.

How a session works
1
Assess
A short baseline test measures how your body responds to lower oxygen, so every session is set to you.
2
Breathe
Rest for around 40 minutes while the mask alternates low-oxygen intervals (like altitude) with high-oxygen recovery intervals.
3
Adapt
Real-time monitoring of heart rate and blood oxygen adjusts each cycle automatically. Your cells respond by clearing old mitochondria and building new, more efficient ones.
Benefits

Short, safe bursts of low oxygen act as a training signal. Over a course of sessions — typically 2–3 a week for 5–8 weeks — the body adapts, and the benefits compound.

✦Energy & stamina
More efficient oxygen use at the cellular level
✦Recovery
Faster bounce-back from training, stress and illness
✦Sleep
Supports breathing regulation and deeper rest
✦Metabolism
Supports insulin sensitivity and healthy weight
✦Resilience
Trains the stress response to recover faster
✦Passive
Seated and relaxed — suitable when exercise isn’t
Who it’s for
Athletes
Altitude-style conditioning while resting — supports aerobic capacity, endurance and faster recovery between sessions.
Sleep apnea
Research suggests IHHT may improve oxygen regulation and sleep quality. Use alongside, never instead of, your prescribed treatment (e.g. CPAP).
Hormones & metabolism
Supports insulin sensitivity, energy and recovery — helpful through menopause, burnout and metabolic change.

Both therapies complement — they don’t replace — medical care. A pre-screening with our nurse confirms suitability; not suitable for everyone (e.g. metal implants, epilepsy, pregnancy, some heart or lung conditions). Talk to your GP or specialist about any diagnosed condition.
