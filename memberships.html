The idea

You are not a collection of symptoms. You are a system — and a system can be tuned.

Your gut shapes your mood. Your stress reshapes your metabolism. Your nervous system governs how you sleep, heal, think and feel. Most wellness manages symptoms one at a time. Membership at THERAPY™ works underneath them — consistently, measurably, and on a rhythm your biology can actually respond to.

Membership terms & the fine print

Billed weekly by direct debit, auto-renewing each term unless 30 days’ written notice is given. All memberships carry a 6-month minimum term. Pause up to 4 weeks per year with notice. All visits pre-booked, subject to availability; 24 hours’ notice to change or cancel. One modality per session, chosen at booking. The Hot/Cold Circuit is 30 minutes total and cannot be a standalone visit. IV Therapy (ELEVATE) is formulated at consultation and subject to clinical suitability. Memberships are non-transferable and not a substitute for medical care. THERAPY™ may adjust inclusions or pricing with reasonable notice.

Memberships are intentionally limited to preserve the calm, quality and personal experience members value. Intake opens quarterly — and it’s open now.
