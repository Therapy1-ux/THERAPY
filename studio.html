Five pillars.
One philosophy.

01

Move

Pilates, mobility, dynamic movement, rhythm and flow. Some sessions are controlled and technical. Others turn the music up. Some are heated. Some aren’t.

Move well. Feel good.

02

Strength

Weights, resistance and bodyweight movement designed to build muscle, stability and physical capacity. No intimidating gym culture. Private 1:1 Speediance training also available.

Build strength for real life.

03

Regulate

Regulation isn’t another word for calm. Breath, somatic movement, rhythm, music, stillness, laughter. Sometimes your nervous system needs to slow down.

Sometimes it needs somewhere for the energy to go.

04

Restore

Slow movement, mobility, breath, sound and deep rest. A space to stop pushing for a while, recover and reset before heading back into life.

Rest is part of training too.

05

Connect

Connection isn’t an extra at THERAPY — it’s part of the point. Classes, coffee, workshops, run clubs, events, guest sessions and familiar faces each week. No forced conversations. No pressure to share.

Just somewhere you can belong.

The immersive studio

One space.
Different states.

The Studio transforms with the experience. Mirrors open when posture and technique matter. Curtains close when they don’t. Projection, dynamic lighting, immersive sound, optional infrared heat and subtle scent move the same room from bright and energetic to warm, dark and deeply restorative.

Train in it.Move in it.Dance in it.Breathe in it.Laugh in it.Rest in it.

The room changes with you.

Curated experiences

The Exhale

A day retreat in Eltham, just 23km from the CBD. Close enough to get to, far enough to switch off.

No two-hour drive, no overnight bag. The Exhale is one guided day that moves you through strength, stillness, nourishment and contrast therapy in a deliberate sequence — each part setting up the next. Small group, expert-led, and designed so you leave calmer, clearer and properly reset.

Arrive

Welcome circle & wellness shot

Phones away, a wellness shot in hand, names learned and the day’s arc laid out. An orientation, not a ritual.

Move

Mat pilates

Control, alignment, breath under effort. The body switched on before the mind is asked to do anything.

Settle

9D meditation

A deeply immersive, multi-layered sound meditation that lets the morning’s work land before you eat.

Nourish

Nourishment lunch

A seasonal, whole-food lunch shared at the table. Fuel for the afternoon, eaten slowly.

Reflect

Mindset & group journalling

Guided prompts, written privately, shared only if you choose. Practical thinking work — patterns, not affirmations.

Release

Somatic & primal movement

Unloaded, instinctive movement that lets the body discharge what the morning surfaced. Coached like training, because it is.

Contrast

Hot & cold immersion

Sauna heat, then the cold plunge. A deliberate stress-and-recover cycle that resets the nervous system and sharpens focus.

Breathe

Immersive breathwork

Guided breathwork with sound and low light, shifting the nervous system from effort into ease.

Close

Closing circle

One intention each to carry out the door. Then you leave — regulated, not raw.

Small group. Held start to finish by THERAPY practitioners. Dates, capacity and inclusions announced with the first release.

Booking & studio policies

A little planning. A better class.

Check in on your phone

Check in through the THERAPY app when you arrive. It helps your instructor greet you by name and know if you’re new.

Arrive on time

Entry closes 1 minute after class starts. The warm-up prepares your body and protects you from injury. A missed class counts as a no-show.

Phone-free classes

Phones stay in lockers or your bag, on silent. On-call carers and Parents & Bubs Pilates are the exception.

More than a studio

The spaces between the appointments matter too.

THERAPY Studio sits inside THERAPY Neurowellness — where movement, mental wellbeing, nervous-system health, recovery, diagnostics and community come together.

Take a class. Stay for breakfast. Have coffee with someone you met inside. Sit by the pond. Add recovery when it suits you. Or simply spend another half hour somewhere you feel welcome.

Because sometimes the conversation afterwards matters just as much as the class that brought you together.

Come as you are
You can care about your health and still have the wine.
You can be strong and still struggle.
You can work too much sometimes. You can miss the workout. You can have a bad week. And you can start again tomorrow.

Wellness isn’t about getting everything right. It’s having things — and people — you can come back to.
