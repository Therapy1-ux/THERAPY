The idea

A room that trains the state, not just the muscle.

Most studios ask you to pick a lane — breath here, strength there, dance somewhere else. THERAPY Studio holds all four in one purpose-built space, sequenced so the room takes you down before it takes you up, and settles you before you leave.

Light, sound, heat and scent are programmed alongside the movement. You don't attend a class here. You enter a state, work inside it, and come out regulated.

Immersive by design

The room is part of the method.

Four environmental layers are programmed to the session, not left to chance — the same discipline we apply to a protocol downstairs.

LIGHT

Circadian-tuned — amber and low at dawn sessions, cool and bright through strength blocks, near-dark for the close.

SOUND

Scored, not shuffled. Tempo carries the arc from arrival through effort to stillness on a spatial system.

HEAT

Infrared-assisted warmth held to the class — hot when the work calls for it, neutral when the breath does.

SCENT

A single house accord, dosed low. Your nervous system learns the room, and settles faster each time you enter it.

A session, end to end

Fifty minutes, four movements.

00 — 08

Arrive

Lights down, breath long. We drop you out of the day before a single rep.

08 — 22

Move

Mobility and rhythm. Dance or flow, depending on the class — the body remembers how to be expressive.

22 — 42

Load

Functional strength under real load, coached and progressed — the part that changes your body composition.

42 — 50

Settle

Breath and stillness in the dark. You leave parasympathetic — calmer than you arrived, not just emptier.

Studio sessions pair with the recovery floor: sauna and ice bath, hydrogen water, and the Outdoor Recovery Lounge in the same visit.
