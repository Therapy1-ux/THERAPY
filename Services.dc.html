Six clear pathways

Care designed as one connected system.

Psychology, gut–brain medicine, allied health, regeneration, diagnostics and hormone health — six pathways built around one idea: your mind, nervous system and gut are a single system. Treat them together and everything improves. Open to the public, one session at a time, or as a guided membership.

01

Allied Health

Registered allied health under the same roof — psychology, myotherapy, registered nurses and functional medicine with our GP, every practitioner AHPRA-registered and accountable to the same clinical standards you'd expect from any medical practice. What changes here is the coordination. Chronic stress doesn't stay in your head: it shows up as a clenched jaw, a locked shoulder, disrupted sleep, a gut that stops behaving. Treated separately, each clinician chases their own symptom. Treated together, your psychologist's work on nervous-system regulation and your myotherapist's work on the tension holding it in place become one plan — with your GP and nurses reading the same notes. Sessions are one-on-one and private, referrals aren't required for most services, and psychology may be Medicare-rebatable with a GP Mental Health Treatment Plan.

Myotherapy→

Assessment-led, hands-on soft-tissue therapy — myofascial release, trigger-point and dry-needling techniques applied to the pattern causing your pain, not just the sore spot. Restores range of movement, settles the guarding that chronic stress creates through the jaw, neck and shoulders, and gives you the loading and mobility work to keep it from returning.

Psychology→

Evidence-based one-on-one psychology for stress, anxiety, low mood, burnout and trauma — drawing on CBT, ACT and nervous-system-informed approaches. Fifty-minute private sessions with an AHPRA-registered psychologist, potentially Medicare-rebatable with a GP Mental Health Treatment Plan, and able to be paired with neuro-suite or bodywork sessions when regulation needs a physical route as well as a conversational one.

Registered Nurses→

Nurse-led infusions, injectables and health support delivered safely in-house. Meet the nurses →

Functional Medicine with our GP→

Whole-person GP care that connects your medical and functional treatments.

Telehealth GP Appointments→

See our GP from anywhere by telehealth — with in-house appointments at THERAPY once a month.

Mental health

AHPRA-registered psychologists deliver evidence-based, often Medicare-rebatable care for stress, anxiety, trauma and low mood — a genuine clinical team, coordinated with your GP and nurses under one roof.

Physical health

Myotherapy, GP and registered-nurse care assess and treat musculoskeletal pain, injury and chronic conditions — releasing soft-tissue tension and restoring pain-free movement and day-to-day function.

03

Gut & Functional Medicine

The gut–brain connection treated clinically. Around 90% of your serotonin is made in the gut, and the vagus nerve carries that conversation straight to your brain — so bloating, fatigue and brain fog are rarely separate problems. Functional medicine, hormone health and restorative infusions rebalance the microbiome, calm intestinal inflammation and correct what your labs actually show, steadying mood and energy from the inside out.

Clinical & Functional Medicine→

Root-cause medicine that treats the gut–brain axis rather than just the symptoms.

Hormone & HRT Programs→

Tailored hormone and HRT support to restore energy, mood and metabolic balance.

IV Drips & Infusions→

Infusions tailored to your bloodwork, not a menu. We test first, read what your labs actually show — iron, B12, vitamin D, magnesium, zinc, amino acids — then a registered nurse formulates and delivers the drip to correct those specific deficiencies. Straight into the bloodstream at full absorption, bypassing a gut that may not be absorbing well in the first place, for genuine gains in energy, immunity, hydration and recovery.

Structured gut-healing plans built around your testing and symptoms.

Mental health

The gut–brain axis governs mood and cognition — around 90% of your serotonin is produced in the gut. Rebalancing the microbiome, calming intestinal inflammation and correcting hormone levels steadies mood, lifts brain fog and builds stress resilience.

Physical health

Root-cause functional medicine targets the drivers behind your symptoms — correcting nutrient deficiencies, hormone imbalance and gut dysfunction to restore digestion, metabolic and immune function and steady, sustained energy.

New · ReOxy breath training

Altitude training, lying down, in forty minutes.

ReOxy delivers intermittent hypoxic–hyperoxic training (IHHT) — you breathe through a mask while the machine alternates air with less oxygen than you'd get on a mountain, then air with more than sea level. The swing is the stimulus. Your body reads the low-oxygen phase as a stress worth adapting to, and the high-oxygen phase gives it the resources to recover before the next round. No effort, no sweat, no impact.

01
Hypoxic phase

Reduced-oxygen air for a few minutes. Weaker, ageing mitochondria fail the test and are cleared; the cells that hold up are the ones that get stronger.

02
Hyperoxic phase

Oxygen-rich air floods back. Circulation opens, repair accelerates, and the body banks the adaptation rather than just surviving the stress.

03
Adaptation

Repeated across a course, the cycle lifts oxygen efficiency, HRV and stress tolerance — the same signal endurance athletes chase at altitude.

Energy & fatigue

Cardio-metabolic
Blood pressure, oxygen uptake and exercise tolerance, trained without load.
Performance
An altitude block for athletes, without leaving Eltham or losing training days.
Measured, not guessed
The device reads your oxygen saturation and heart rate live and tunes each cycle to you.
