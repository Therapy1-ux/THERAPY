Gift voucher terms

Vouchers are valid for 3 years from the date of purchase and can be redeemed against any THERAPY treatment, membership or retail product.

Vouchers are not redeemable for cash and cannot be exchanged or refunded. Any unused balance remains on the voucher for future visits.

Digital vouchers are delivered by email; physical vouchers are printed and held for collection in clinic. Please treat physical vouchers like cash — lost or stolen cards can't be replaced.

Bookings for treatments remain subject to consultation and clinical suitability. To redeem, present your voucher code online at checkout or to our concierge in clinic.
