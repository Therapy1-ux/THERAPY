Corporate · the thesis

The nervous system at work.

Work asks a great deal of the nervous system — deadlines, noise, travel, screens, and the low constant hum of being always reachable. Most corporate wellbeing meets this at the level of the mind: a talk, an app, a subscription nobody opens. THERAPY™ works at the level of the body, because the nervous system does not respond to information. It responds to input — cold, heat, infrared, pressure, breath, frequency, oxygen, food.

II · ExoMind™ workplace program

The clinic comes to you.

ExoMind™ delivers targeted transcranial magnetic stimulation — a clinical device, delivered by a qualified consultant to individually screened participants. A course runs eight to ten sessions across a year, twenty-four minutes each. Delivered on your premises, a participant loses twenty-four minutes rather than half a day travelling to a clinic.

How it is delivered
Minimum six participants. Every participant is individually screened before their first session — the device is not suitable for everyone.
Delivered by a qualified consultant, present throughout.
Each participant receives a personal between-sessions guide — sleep and light timing, breath practice, movement, and what to record.
Participation is voluntary and confidential. The employer is not told who took part.
A private, quiet room on site, and one THERAPY™ contact for the program.

A clinical program, not a wellbeing activation. THERAPY™ makes no claim that it treats any condition; suitability is determined at screening.

VI · High performance

Nervous system training for your athletes.

Strength and conditioning trains the engine. Almost nobody trains the system that decides whether the engine is available — autonomic state, sleep quality, stress load, the ability to drop into parasympathetic between efforts. That is what this building does, and it is built as one structure rather than six services: contrast therapy, cryotherapy, compression, hyperbaric oxygen, ReOxy altitude training and diagnostics under one roof, on one protocol, reading from one set of numbers. Squad blocks run outside public hours on a standing weekly slot, coordinated with the performance and medical staff you already have.

01
Downshift & turnaround

Contrast therapy, cryotherapy, compression and infrared used to force a parasympathetic shift after load — so the athlete sleeps that night, not two nights later.

02
Readiness, measured

HRV, sleep, bloodwork and body composition tracked across a squad, so autonomic state becomes a selection input rather than a feeling. Reported to your performance staff; the athlete holds their own results.

03
Stress tolerance, trained

ReOxy IHHT and hyperbaric oxygen programmed as a course — controlled hypoxic stress that lifts oxygen efficiency and HRV. An altitude camp without losing training days.

Who this is built for
