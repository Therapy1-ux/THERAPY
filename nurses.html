Cannulation is the gentle insertion of a fine, flexible tube (a cannula) into a vein, so fluids, infusions or medication can be given safely — then removed when you're done. Nurse-led care simply means a registered nurse manages the whole process: assessing you beforehand, delivering the treatment, and monitoring how you respond throughout.

A test that measures how your body handles sugar over about two hours — a fasting blood sample, a sweet glucose drink, then follow-up samples. Who it's for: pregnant women screening for gestational diabetes, and anyone checking for pre-diabetes, insulin resistance, or the cause of unexplained fatigue, thirst or blood-sugar swings. Why: it catches blood-sugar problems early, when they're easiest to act on. Come fasted — our nurse guides you through every step.

Routine blood tests, vitals and health screening collected on-site by our nurses and sent to an accredited lab — from iron studies and cholesterol to hormones and general wellness panels. A simple way to know your baseline before starting any pathway.

Nurse & Doctor only treatments

Considered care, delivered by registered professionals.

AHPRA-registered practitioners
Every treatment is performed by a nurse or doctor registered with the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency.

At THERAPY, we prioritise a thorough consultation and assessment process to support informed decision-making. During your consultation, your practitioner will discuss your concerns, relevant medical history, and lifestyle factors, and provide information about available treatment options, including potential risks and considerations.

New clients are asked to allow approximately 45–60 minutes for an individual consultation to ensure sufficient time for discussion and assessment.

Full and accurate disclosure of your medical history, previous treatments, medications, and lifestyle factors is essential. While treatments offered at THERAPY are elective and cosmetic in nature, some procedures involve skin penetration or other invasive techniques. Certain medical conditions, medications, or lifestyle factors may affect treatment suitability, risk profile, or whether a treatment can proceed. Your practitioner will assess this information to determine appropriateness and discuss relevant considerations with you.

All personal and medical information is handled confidentially. If you choose to bring a friend, family member, or partner to your appointment, please be aware that medical and personal information may be discussed in their presence.
